# Confidence Model

## Levels

| Level | Meaning | Typical Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| High | Directly confirmed or strongly cross-validated | Official document; multiple independent reliable sources |
| Medium | Credible but incomplete | Reliable third-party source; partial primary evidence |
| Low | Reasonable engineering inference | Photos, teardown, indirect specifications |
| Unknown | Insufficient evidence | Contradictory or missing information |

## Rules

- Confidence describes the evidence for the claim, not how plausible the claim sounds.
- A technically plausible inference is still Low confidence until verified.
- Estimated cost and BOM figures should normally be Medium or Low unless backed by detailed evidence.
- When sources conflict, preserve the conflict and explain why one source is preferred.
