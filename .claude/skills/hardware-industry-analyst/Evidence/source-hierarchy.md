# Source Hierarchy

## Priority

### Tier 1 — Primary
- Official product pages
- Technical documentation
- User / service manuals
- Official Wiki
- Regulatory / certification documents
- Patents
- Official presentations and technical talks

### Tier 2 — High-quality secondary
- Reputable industry media
- Professional product reviews
- Engineering teardown reports
- Company interviews

### Tier 3 — Community / supply chain
- Engineering forums
- Specialist communities
- Supplier information
- Component databases

### Tier 4 — Discovery only
- Search engine snippets
- Aggregator pages
- Unsourced social posts

Tier 4 can discover leads but should not be the final evidence for important claims.

## Source Selection Rules

1. Prefer primary sources for specifications and architecture.
2. Use multiple independent sources for disputed claims.
3. Prefer contemporaneous sources for price, release status, and software features.
4. For teardown-based conclusions, preserve the distinction between observed hardware and inferred function.
5. Do not cite a source for a claim that the source does not actually support.
