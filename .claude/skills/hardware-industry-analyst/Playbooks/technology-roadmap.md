# Technology Roadmap Playbook

## Objective
Infer the evolution of a hardware technology from historical products, technical disclosures and competitive implementations.

## Workflow
1. Define the technology boundary.
2. Build a historical timeline.
3. Track architecture, components, algorithms, materials and manufacturing changes.
4. Identify performance bottlenecks in each generation.
5. Map competing technical routes.
6. Distinguish observed roadmap signals from inference.
7. Build Base / Accelerated / Disrupted scenarios.
8. Identify implications for product and company strategy.
