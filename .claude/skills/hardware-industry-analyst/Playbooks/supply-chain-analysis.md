# Supply Chain Analysis Playbook

## Objective
Understand how product architecture, component sourcing, manufacturing geography and supplier concentration affect cost, quality, lead time and competitive advantage.

## Workflow
1. Decompose the product into critical assemblies.
2. Identify known suppliers and component manufacturers from primary and credible secondary evidence.
3. Classify components by strategic importance and substitutability.
4. Map manufacturing and assembly stages.
5. Assess vertical integration versus outsourcing.
6. Identify bottlenecks, single-source risks and qualification barriers.
7. Connect supply-chain choices to product architecture and economics.
8. State unknowns explicitly.

## Rules
- Do not infer supplier identity solely from industry-common component choices.
- Distinguish component maker, distributor, contract manufacturer and brand owner.
- Treat supplier information as time-sensitive.
