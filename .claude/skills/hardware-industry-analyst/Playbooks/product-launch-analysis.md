# Product Launch Analysis Playbook

## Objective
Analyze why a product was launched, what changed technically, and how it affects the company's portfolio and competition.

## Workflow
1. Establish launch date and announced positioning.
2. Compare previous generation / closest predecessor.
3. Extract confirmed specification changes.
4. Map architecture and subsystem changes.
5. Identify new software / algorithm / sensor / electronics capabilities.
6. Separate real engineering changes from marketing changes.
7. Assess target-user and price-band movement.
8. Evaluate competitive implications and likely next products.

## Output
Use a before/after matrix plus a short explanation of the business and technical significance.
