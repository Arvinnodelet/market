# Financial Analysis Framework

## Objective

Analyze the economic performance and sustainability of a hardware business. Used for IPO evaluation, competitive benchmarking, and identifying structural advantages or vulnerabilities.

---

# 1. Revenue Structure

| Revenue Stream | What to Look For | Data Sources |
|---|---|---|
| **Hardware** | Unit sales trend, ASP, product mix (entry vs premium) | Earnings reports, shipment data, retail pricing |
| **Consumables** | Attach rate, lock-in %, recurring ratio | Company filings, ecosystem analysis |
| **Software / Cloud** | Subscription revenue, premium memberships, marketplace commissions | App store data, company disclosures |
| **Services** | Enterprise contracts, extended warranty, professional services | Press releases, case studies |

**Green flags**: Consumables >20% of revenue (recurring). ASP rising YoY. Multiple revenue streams.
**Red flags**: >80% hardware-dependent. ASP declining. Revenue concentration (1 product = >50%).

---

# 2. Cost Structure

| Cost Line | % of Revenue (Typical Consumer Hardware) | What to Assess |
|---|---|---|
| **COGS** | 55–75% | Vertical integration reduces this; contract mfg increases it |
| **R&D** | 5–12% | Below 5% = underinvesting. Above 15% = burning cash (unless hypergrowth) |
| **Sales & Marketing** | 8–20% | Above 18% = potentially desperate. Below 8% = strong brand pull |
| **G&A** | 5–10% | Public company costs (IPO fees, compliance) inflate this temporarily |
| **Logistics** | 5–10% | Tariff exposure; warehouse footprint efficiency |

---

# 3. Gross Margin Analysis

| Product Tier | Typical Gross Margin | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Entry-level | 15–25% | Volume play, low margin |
| Mid-range | 25–35% | Sweet spot for consumer brands |
| Premium | 35–50% | Brand and features command premium |
| Consumables | 40–60% | The profit engine — lock-in protects this |
| Industrial / Professional | 40–60% | Low volume, high margin |

**Red flag**: Gross margin declining 3+ quarters straight (pricing pressure).
**Green flag**: Gross margin expanding (brand power, vertical integration paying off).

---

# 4. Unit Economics

| Metric | How to Calculate | Healthy Range |
|---|---|---|
| **ASP (Average Selling Price)** | Hardware revenue ÷ units shipped | Trending up = good; down = commoditization |
| **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)** | S&M spend ÷ new customers | Declining = brand working |
| **LTV (Lifetime Value)** | ASP + (annual consumable spend × retention years) | >3× CAC is strong |
| **Consumable Attach Rate** | Proprietary consumables sold ÷ active devices | Healthy if >10 units/year = ecosystem working |

---

# 5. Capital & Investment

| Metric | What to Assess | Red Flags |
|---|---|---|
| **R&D Intensity** | R&D ÷ Revenue | Below 3% = harvesting, not investing |
| **Capex** | Factory, tooling, automation investment | Flat while revenue grows = capacity crunch coming |
| **Cash Runway** | Cash ÷ Monthly burn | <12 months without clear path to profit |
| **Inventory Days** | Inventory ÷ Daily COGS | Rising = demand softening or supply chain issues |

---

# 6. Growth Drivers

| Driver | Signal Strength | How to Verify |
|---|---|---|
| **New product launches** | Revenue bump at launch quarter | Track launch cycles vs revenue |
| **Geographic expansion** | New market entry → distribution build | Country-level revenue breakdown |
| **Ecosystem monetization** | Consumables + subscriptions growing faster than hardware | Recurring revenue ratio |
| **Adjacent categories** | New product categories, accessories, services | New category revenue contribution |

---

# 7. Financial Risks

| Risk | Severity | Indicators |
|---|---|---|
| **Pricing war** | 🔴 High | Multiple competitors dropping prices 10%+ in same tier |
| **Tariff exposure** | 🔴 High | Revenue concentration in high-tariff regions |
| **Inventory write-down** | 🟠 Medium | New model launches making old stock obsolete |
| **Currency risk** | 🟠 Medium | Revenue in USD/EUR, costs in RMB — RMB appreciation hurts |
| **IPO overhang** | 🟡 Monitor | Lock-up expiry → insider selling pressure |
| **Key supplier dependency** | 🔴 High | Single-source chips/components |
