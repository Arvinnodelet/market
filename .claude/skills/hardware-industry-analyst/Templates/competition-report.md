# Competition Report Template

## 1. Competitive Boundary
Define product/category/region/time period.

## 2. Player Map
| Player | Core products | Technology route | Price band | Channel | Position |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 3. Product Matrix
| Dimension | Player A | Player B | Player C |
|---|---|---|---|

## 4. Technology Comparison
Compare architectures, key components, algorithms, materials and manufacturing choices.

## 5. Ecosystem Comparison
Software, consumables, accessories, cloud, APIs, community and switching costs.

## 6. Business / Manufacturing Comparison
Pricing, channels, supply chain, vertical integration, service and cost structure.

## 7. Competitive Dynamics
Sources of advantage, weaknesses, barriers, likely responses and market shifts.

## 8. Strategic Assessment
Identify the dimensions where competition is actually decided and why.
