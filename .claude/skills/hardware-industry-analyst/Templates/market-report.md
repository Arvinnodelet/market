# Market Report Template

## 1. Market Definition
Define included/excluded products, geography, customer segments and time period.

## 2. Market Size
| Metric | Year | Value | Source | Confidence |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|

Use TAM/SAM/SOM only when definitions and data support them.

## 3. Demand Structure
Customer segments, use cases, purchase drivers, replacement cycle and adoption barriers.

## 4. Channel Structure
Direct, distributors, marketplaces, retail, system integrators and service channels.

## 5. Competitive Structure
Player map, share where supported, price bands, product coverage and differentiation.

## 6. Technology Structure
Technology routes, maturity, cost/performance curves and disruptive alternatives.

## 7. Regional Structure
Compare regions by demand, regulation, channel, local players and economics.

## 8. Forecast
Base / Upside / Downside scenarios with explicit assumptions.

## 9. Key Drivers & Risks
Separate observable drivers from analytical assumptions.

## 10. Market Conclusions
Market attractiveness, structural changes, winning capabilities and implications for companies/products.
