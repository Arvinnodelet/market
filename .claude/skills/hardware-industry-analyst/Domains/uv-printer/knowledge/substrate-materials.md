# UV Printer Substrate & Materials Knowledge

## 1. Why Substrate Matters

UV ink is cured on the substrate surface, so final performance depends on both ink chemistry and substrate properties.

Important variables:
- Surface energy
- Surface roughness
- Porosity
- Coating
- Cleanliness
- Flexibility
- Thermal sensitivity
- Chemical resistance

## 2. Wetting

A droplet must wet the surface sufficiently without uncontrolled spreading.

Poor wetting can cause:
- Pinholes
- Poor coverage
- Weak adhesion
- Irregular edges

Excessive spreading can reduce:
- Fine detail
- Edge definition
- Color density control

## 3. Adhesion

Adhesion depends on:
- Surface chemistry
- Surface energy
- Ink formulation
- Cure state
- Mechanical interlocking
- Surface treatment
- Coating compatibility

A good-looking print is not evidence of good adhesion.

## 4. Typical Material Categories

Research may cover:
- Acrylic / PMMA
- Polycarbonate
- PVC
- PET
- ABS
- Coated metals
- Glass
- Ceramic
- Wood
- Leather / synthetic leather
- Paper / board
- Films

Actual compatibility must be verified for the specific ink and printer.

## 5. Surface Treatment

Possible treatments include:
- Cleaning
- Corona treatment
- Plasma treatment
- Primer
- Coating
- Mechanical preparation

Treatment can improve adhesion but adds process cost and complexity.

## 6. Material Flatness

Printhead-to-substrate distance can be critical.

Analyze:
- Bed flatness
- Fixture repeatability
- Material warpage
- Thickness tolerance
- Height sensing
- Z-axis adjustment

For rigid desktop systems, substrate height variation can become a practical print-quality limitation.

## 7. Thermal Effects

UV curing produces heat, and some substrates are temperature-sensitive.

Potential effects:
- Warping
- Dimensional change
- Coating damage
- Adhesive failure

## 8. Durability Testing

Useful tests include:
- Cross-hatch adhesion
- Scratch resistance
- Abrasion
- Chemical resistance
- UV/weathering exposure
- Bending / flexing

When reports cite durability, identify the test method rather than treating generic claims such as “durable” as quantified evidence.

## 9. Research Indicators

Look for:
- Supported substrate list
- Ink-specific material compatibility
- Primer recommendations
- Adhesion test data
- Surface preparation requirements
- Maximum substrate thickness
- Maximum substrate weight
- Bed / fixture architecture
- Height tolerance
