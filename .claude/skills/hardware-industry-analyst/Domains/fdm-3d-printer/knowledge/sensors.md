# Sensor Knowledge Base


## Purpose

This document summarizes sensor technologies commonly used in modern 3D printers.

The focus is engineering tradeoffs, not marketing claims.

---

# Eddy Current Sensor

## Principle

Measures displacement through changes in electromagnetic field coupling between the probe and conductive target.

## Advantages

* High repeatability
* Fast response
* No mechanical contact
* Good for automatic bed leveling
* Long service life

## Disadvantages

* Requires conductive targets
* Sensitive to material type
* Higher cost than inductive probes

## Typical Applications

* Bambu Lab H2D
* Industrial positioning systems
* CNC measurement systems

## Evaluation

Accuracy: High

Reliability: High

Cost: Medium

Manufacturing Complexity: Medium

---

# Inductive Sensor

## Principle

Detects metal objects through electromagnetic induction.

## Advantages

* Low cost
* Mature technology
* Easy integration

## Disadvantages

* Lower accuracy
* Limited sensing distance
* Material dependent

## Evaluation

Accuracy: Medium

Reliability: High

Cost: Low

Manufacturing Complexity: Low

---

# Load Cell

## Principle

Measures force through deformation of elastic structures.

## Advantages

* Direct nozzle contact sensing
* High precision
* Independent of bed material

## Disadvantages

* Mechanical complexity
* Calibration requirements
* Structural design challenges

## Typical Applications

* Prusa MK4
* Prusa XL

## Evaluation

Accuracy: High

Reliability: Medium

Cost: Medium

Manufacturing Complexity: High

---

# Strain Gauge

## Principle

Measures strain generated by mechanical deformation.

## Advantages

* Compact
* Low cost
* Flexible integration

## Disadvantages

* Sensitive to mounting conditions
* Temperature effects

## Evaluation

Accuracy: Medium

Reliability: Medium

Cost: Low

Manufacturing Complexity: Medium

---

# LiDAR

## Principle

Measures distance using laser reflection.

## Advantages

* Non-contact
* Rich surface information
* Can support first-layer inspection

## Disadvantages

* High cost
* Environmental sensitivity
* Complex algorithms

## Typical Applications

* Bambu X1 Series

## Evaluation

Accuracy: High

Reliability: Medium

Cost: High

Manufacturing Complexity: High

---

# AI Vision System

## Principle

Uses cameras and machine learning for defect detection.

## Applications

* Spaghetti detection
* First layer inspection
* Print monitoring

## Advantages

* Rich information
* Continuous improvement through software

## Disadvantages

* Computational cost
* False positives
* Training requirements

## Evaluation

Accuracy: Medium to High

Reliability: Medium

Cost: Medium

Manufacturing Complexity: Medium

# Selection Guide

| Scenario                  | Recommended Sensor                |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Low Cost Consumer Printer | Inductive                         |
| Prosumer Printer          | Eddy Current                      |
| Premium Consumer Printer  | Eddy Current + Vision             |
| Industrial Printer        | Eddy Current + Vision + Load Cell |
| Research Platform         | Load Cell + Vision                |

# Industry Trend

2020-2023

* Inductive dominant
* Load Cell emerging

2023-2025

* LiDAR adoption
* AI vision adoption

2025+

Expected trend:

* Eddy Current replacing traditional probes
* Vision becoming standard
* Sensor fusion becoming mainstream
