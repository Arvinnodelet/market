# FDM Mechanical Structure

## Purpose

Evaluate frame, gantry, bed and structural interfaces as the mechanical foundation of print accuracy and speed.

## Main Elements

- frame
- gantry
- toolhead mount
- bed support
- Z structure
- linear motion supports
- belt and screw interfaces
- enclosure

## Key Properties

- stiffness
- mass distribution
- alignment
- orthogonality
- vibration damping
- thermal stability
- assembly tolerance

## Performance Chain

```text
Frame Stiffness
     ↓
Structural Deformation
     ↓
Motion Error
     ↓
Layer Registration
     ↓
Dimensional / Surface Quality
```

## Research Indicators

- frame construction
- extrusion size and geometry
- gantry support
- bed support structure
- structural symmetry
- fastener strategy
- cable management
- service access

## Research Rules

Do not judge rigidity from material or frame appearance alone. Consider load path, moving mass, support span, joints and measured vibration behavior.
