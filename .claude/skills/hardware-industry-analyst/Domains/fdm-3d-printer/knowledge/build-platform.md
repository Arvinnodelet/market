# FDM Build Platform

## Purpose

Analyze the print surface and bed system that establishes first-layer geometry, adhesion and thermal boundary conditions.

## Components

- heated bed
- build plate
- spring steel / flexible plate
- PEI and coated surfaces
- magnetic attachment
- bed support
- insulation
- bed mesh / probing interface

## Key Variables

- flatness
- thermal uniformity
- heating rate
- adhesion
- surface durability
- plate rigidity
- removal force
- thermal expansion

## First-Layer Chain

```text
Bed Geometry
   + Z Reference
   + Temperature
   + Surface Energy
   + Material
        ↓
First-Layer Deposition
        ↓
Adhesion + Geometry
        ↓
Print Reliability
```

## Research Indicators

- bed size
- heater power
- temperature range
- measured uniformity
- plate material/coating
- automatic probing method
- replacement cost
- wear and cleaning requirements

## Research Rules

Treat advertised flatness, measured flatness and first-layer performance as different evidence types.
